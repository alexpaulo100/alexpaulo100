## 📌 About Me

Hi! I work in Data Engineering, Systems Development, and Process Automation, leveraging **Python, SQL, Oracle, and PostgreSQL** to turn complex business needs into high-performance, integrated solutions.

I design and maintain end-to-end applications connecting core databases, APIs, and ERP systems—specifically **Systêxtil (Oracle DB)**—handling the full lifecycle from architecture to production deployment on Linux servers.

---

### 🚀 Key Projects & Expertise

- 🔄 **E-commerce & ERP Integrations:** Resilient connectors between Mercado Livre and Systêxtil ERP built with Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Oracle, PostgreSQL, Celery, Redis, and Docker.
- 📦 **Import Management System:** Full-Stack corporate software for yarn import tracking, integrating Oracle/Systêxtil, Active Directory, PCP, finance, auditing, and operational alerts.
- 🏦 **Banking & Reconciliation Automation:** Automated routines for financial reconciliation (Bradesco & Santander), integrating bank files directly with the ERP to reduce manual workload.
- 🛢️ **Data Engineering & ETL:** Building ETL pipelines and Data Warehouse structures from ERP data to ensure governance, reliability, and automated updates.
- 📊 **Executive Dashboards & Observability (OCI + Streamlit):** Analytical apps and synchronization pipelines integrated with Systêxtil ERP, hosted on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI / Linux) with automated data-freshness testing.
- 📈 **Demand Forecasting:** Data solutions analyzing sales history and fashion collection curves to project demand and perform backtesting for production planning (PCP).
- 🛠️ **DevOps & Performance Tuning:** Database optimization (Oracle DB/PL-SQL tuning) and application containerization with Docker on Linux servers.
